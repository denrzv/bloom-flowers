Why there is no checkout

SiteSkin describes navigation and branding. It never gains access to what a page contains, what a visitor types, or what a shop stores — so a demonstration of the protocol needs no commerce behind it, and adding one would only suggest the protocol reaches further than it does.

Bloom Flowers — a demonstration site for the SiteSkin protocol. Nothing here is for sale.

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests. Every byte is served from this origin.
